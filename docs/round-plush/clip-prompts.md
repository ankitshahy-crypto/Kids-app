# Clip prompts: the other eleven
The dog's clip brief (README.md, section 2) for each of the other eleven animals, one block per clip,
for the same video generator that made the dog's idle and cheer. Each block is self-contained: paste
it alone. Name the file as its heading says (`animal-<id>-<clip>.mp4`); `scripts/animal-clips.py`
reads the id and the clip from that name.
Every clip: 24 fps, square, camera locked, the animal whole and centered with air around it, on the
flat even green `#5aa85a`, no props, no text, no watermark, no shadow on the ground. The first and the
last frame are the idle pose, so the idle loops and any move hands back to it. Lengths: idle 8 s;
cheer about 1 s; walk, jump, spin, dance and sing about 0.7 s each, one action in place, then back to
idle. The animal's own sentence and appendage come from the table in README.md: the frog's eyes sit on
top of the head and are its appendage; the cat keeps its tabby stripes and white muzzle. The faces are
the shipped stills' faces; nothing here changes a still. The dog is not here: its clips are made.
