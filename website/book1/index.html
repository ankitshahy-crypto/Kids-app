Grown-ups: five things that help

  • Say the sound, not the name. m says “mmm”, a says “a” as in apple. Hold the sounds you can stretch: “mmmm”, “sss”.
  • Sound it out together. Words in bold use only the sounds learned so far. Point, stretch each sound, then say it fast: aaa-mmm, am!
  • Read-together words. Little words like I, a, the, is, look and names like Sam, Tam, Pip are read whole. Read them for your child; they will pick them up.
  • 5 to 10 minutes a day. A letter page, a tracing page, a find-the-letter page, then a little story. Stop while it is still fun.
  • Friday is review day. In the book, a review page and a star to color. In the app, a short sound game and a star.
